How to Make Maggi

Ingredients

  • Maggi Noodles
  • 2 Cups of Water
  • Maggi Masala

Steps

  1. Boil 2 cups of water.
  2. Add the Maggi noodles.
  3. Add the Maggi masala.
  4. Cook for 2–3 minutes.
  5. Serve hot.
